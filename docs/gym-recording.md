# Recording footage at the gym

A checklist for the first recording session. The footage becomes the Phase 2 development set: it's used to measure the make detector and to fine-tune it.

A shoot-around is fine for this first session. A real game is still needed later, to see players blocking the view, find shooters in a crowd, and detect when a game starts.

## Before you go

- [ ] **Free storage:** 4K/30 is about 170 MB per minute, so 30 minutes is about 5 GB. Leave room for 40 minutes.
- [ ] **Charge the battery pack** and bring a cable. Bring the light stand and phone mount.
- [ ] **Set the Camera app to 4K at 30 fps:** Settings → Camera → Record Video → 4K at 30 fps. Use 30 fps, not 60, because 30 is what the browser gets, so the footage matches what the app will see.

## Set up

- [ ] **Placement:** stand at the midcourt sideline, as far back from the line as the gym allows, ideally about 10 ft.
- [ ] **Height:** raise the phone to 7–8 ft.
- [ ] **Camera app:** Video mode, **0.5×**, Action mode off.
- [ ] **Framing:**
  - [ ] both rims and both nets are fully in frame, with some space above each rim
  - [ ] the floor around both 3-point lines is visible
- [ ] **Exposure:** press and hold on the court until "AE/AF LOCK" appears, so the brightness doesn't keep changing as players move.
- [ ] **Note the landmarks:** something near each basket (e.g. door side, window side), and which 3-point line is yours if the floor has more than one.

## While recording

- [ ] **Record 20–40 minutes**, aiming for **100+ makes** across both baskets. One long recording or several clips are both fine; don't trim or edit them.
- [ ] **Shoot at both baskets.**
- [ ] **Shoot 2s and 3s in runs,** e.g. 10 from inside the arc, then 10 from outside. Or say "three" out loud on each 3.
- [ ] **Mix in the hard cases:** swishes, layups, rattle-ins, rim-outs, bank shots, and airballs that pass in front of or behind the net.
- [ ] **Keep the phone still.** If it gets bumped or moved, note the time, or start a new clip.
- [ ] **A busy gym is good.** Other courts and balls in the background are realistic and worth seeing.

## Afterwards

- [ ] **Feel the phone.** If it got hot, mention it, because the real app will run under the same conditions.
- [ ] **AirDrop the original videos to the Mac.** Keep the originals, not compressed copies.
- [ ] **Note which clips share a camera position.**
- [ ] **Label each video** at `/label/` ([labeler guide](labeler.md)) and export the JSON next to the video.
